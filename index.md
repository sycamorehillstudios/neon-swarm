# Neon Swarm: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. Sycamore Hill Studios ("we") makes Neon Swarm: Idle Swipe Shooter ("the game"). This page says what data the game handles and why.

## No accounts, no servers of our own

The game has no sign-in and we run no server for it. Your progress is saved on your device. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, your progress is deleted.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game only shows an ad when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching one. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects and shares data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. See [how Google uses data from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are handled by Google Play. We receive a record that a purchase was made so the game can deliver it; we never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment.

## Reminders

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules notifications on your device (for example, when your free gift is ready). They are created on the device; nothing is sent to us.

## What we don't do

We don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we don't use any analytics service of our own. We don't sell personal data.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
